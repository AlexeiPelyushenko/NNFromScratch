--------------------------------------------------------------------- Conclusion ---------------------------------------------------------------------

It's disheartening to see that the more fancy activation functions (GELU, SiLU) underperform here compared to very simple ones like ReLU and leaky ReLU. Though, after
some research, that seems to be expected. GELU and SiLU were made for deep, large neural networks where smooth gating helps with optimization on a much larger scale.
The networks I'm building for the linear regression task in median house prices are very simple, and don't require complex activation functions to begin with. It's like
using a race car to get groceries. Also sigmoid is not good for this task.
